About IIT Delhi Virtual Labs

Virtual Labs is a landmark project under the National Mission on Education through Information and Communication Technology (NMEICT). Virtual Labs Feedback Guidelines
1. Purpose of Feedback

IIT Delhi Virtual Labs aims to provide flawless academic simulation tools across colleges nationwide. Your feedback is reviewed by domain subject coordinators and web engineering developers.

2. Constructive Bug Reporting

When reporting errors or simulation failures, please identify the step where the experiment failed, your browser model, and attach a screenshot of the console or simulator canvas.

3. Screenshot Guidelines
  • Do not upload screenshots containing sensitive passwords, banking details, or confidential personal information.
  • Highlight the error banner or miscalculated graphs where applicable.
  • Accepted formats: PNG, JPG, JPEG, WEBP up to 10 MB each.
4. Follow-up Procedure

Every submission generates a unique tracking reference code (`IITD-VL-2026-XXXXXX`). You can cite this reference ID when contacting your institution's Virtual Labs nodal center.

Privacy & Data Handling Notice

IIT Delhi Virtual Labs is committed to data privacy and responsible academic information handling.

  • Personal Data: Contact details (name, email, phone) are strictly used for diagnostic follow-up and are never shared with commercial third parties.
  • Anonymous Submission: If you select 'Submit Anonymously', no personal identifiers or IP contacts are linked to your feedback record.
  • Screenshots: Uploaded images are stored in protected directories accessible solely to authenticated administrators and system developers.
